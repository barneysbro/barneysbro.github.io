- **EN summary:** Live arrivals across Hong Kong’s major operators, with nearby stops, full route maps, favourites, widgets, and direct or one-transfer journey planning.
- **Verified features:** major operator coverage; live arrivals; nearby stops and route maps; direct/one-transfer planning with fare and walking comparisons; favourites/groups and history; home-screen widgets; Traditional Chinese and English.
