# Homepage surface brief

- Scope: `index.html` portfolio/marketing homepage; visitor mode: Persuade.
- Audience: people evaluating a focused city utility and existing users navigating to support/legal destinations.
- Job: understand which App serves their city and task, see real product evidence, then open the correct App Store page.
- Proof: verified feature copy, official-data sources, five App icons, and real screenshots. No ratings, download totals, testimonials, or invented claims.
- Constraints: preserve all existing routes and `#home`, `#apps`, `#about`, `#contact`; static HTML/CSS/JS; Traditional Chinese and English; keyboard, reduced motion, and narrow mobile support.
- Chosen direction: The City Route Atlas—full-field municipal colors, hard civic rules, oversized wayfinding type, screenshot plates, and a scroll-linked route.
- Memorable moment: the first viewport establishes a live city route, then each full-width destination changes the route color while proving one App.
- Unresolved: none required for release; future App UI changes may refresh individual screenshot derivatives.
