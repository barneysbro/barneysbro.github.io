# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript deployed with GitHub Pages. Preserve this stack unless a future requirement proves it insufficient.

## Users

- People looking for a focused iOS tool for daily transport, waste collection, traffic-camera checks, or reusable text.
- Existing App users who follow support, privacy, terms, and lost-property links from inside an App.
- Prospective users evaluating whether each App covers their city and daily task before opening the App Store.

## Product Purpose

Barneysbro is an independent App studio by Barney Chen. The website explains a portfolio of seven practical iOS utilities, shows how each one reduces friction in a recurring daily task, and directs visitors to the correct App Store product page or support resource.

## Positioning

One independent studio turns fragmented information and repeated daily actions into focused native tools: when the bus arrives, where waste is collected, what the road looks like, or which saved reply to send.

## Operating Context

Visitors often arrive on mobile, may be outside or in transit, and need to scan quickly. Existing App webviews and external links depend on stable public routes. The portfolio spans Traditional Chinese and English content.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Portfolio: HKBUS, 台北倒垃圾, TPEBUS, SGBUS, LONDONBUS, RainCam, and Quick Copy.
- The homepage supports Traditional Chinese and English.
- Existing routes and homepage fragments are a compatibility contract; see `docs/tasks/baseline/README.md`.
- Product claims must be supported by source repositories or current App Store listings.
- Legal text and lost-property contact information must keep their meaning.
- Interactions and motion must remain usable with keyboard, touch, JavaScript failure, and reduced-motion preferences.
- No framework or animation dependency is needed for the current scope.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Barneysbro.
- Independent studio voice: direct, useful, locally informed, and product-led rather than corporate.
- The user requested a contemporary startup-industry website with interaction and scroll animation.
- Existing App icons and real App screenshots remain the primary visual evidence.

## Evidence on Hand

- Seven App icons and product screenshots under `images/`.
- Current App Store descriptions and product URLs recorded in `docs/app-content.md`.

## Product Principles

1. Show the real product before making claims about it.
2. Make each city and daily job immediately distinguishable.
3. Prefer current official-data facts over marketing superlatives.
4. Keep support and legal destinations dependable for existing users.
5. Make motion clarify the journey through the portfolio, never delay it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must meet practical WCAG AA expectations: semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast, meaningful image alternatives, 200% zoom resilience, and an intentional reduced-motion experience.
